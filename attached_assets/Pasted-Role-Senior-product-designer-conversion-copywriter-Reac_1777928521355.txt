Role: Senior product designer + conversion copywriter + React/Expo/Replit engineer.

Context:
We are building the first landing page for Norovia, a calm daily support app for people who experience POTS-like symptoms, chronic fatigue, dizziness, palpitations, brain fog, or fluctuating energy.

Norovia is not medical care. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace a clinician. It helps users understand their daily body state and respond with pacing, hydration, rest, and planning guidance.

Positioning line:
“Most tools track your body. Norovia helps you understand what your body is telling you — and how to respond.”

Goal:
Create a clean, mobile-first landing page to validate interest and collect beta/waitlist signups.

Tone:
Calm, validating, modern, minimal, not cheesy, not overly clinical, not wellness-guru.
Avoid words like “cure,” “treat,” “fix,” “diagnose,” “optimize.”
Do not overpromise.

Page structure:

1. Hero section
Headline:
“Understand your body state before the day runs you.”

Subheadline:
“Norovia turns a quick daily check-in into a simple Today State, so you can pace, plan, and respond with more clarity.”

Primary CTA:
“Join the beta waitlist”

Secondary text:
“Built for people navigating fluctuating energy, dizziness, palpitations, brain fog, and low reserve days.”

2. Problem section
Title:
“When your symptoms fluctuate, planning your day gets harder.”

Body:
“Some days your energy looks fine, but your body says otherwise. Norovia helps you notice the pattern earlier and adjust before you overextend.”

3. Three feature cards
Card 1:
Title: “Daily check-in”
Text: “Log energy, symptoms, sleep, hydration, and optional vitals in under a minute.”

Card 2:
Title: “Today State”
Text: “See whether today is steady, mindful, take-it-easy, or flare-mode based on your inputs.”

Card 3:
Title: “Gentle daily plan”
Text: “Get simple pacing suggestions for how to move through the day without pushing blindly.”

4. Flare mode section
Title:
“For the days that are different.”

Text:
“When symptoms are higher, Flare Mode makes the priority clear: stabilize first, reduce demands, and know when to seek urgent help.”

Include safety line:
“If symptoms feel severe, unusual, or concerning, seek medical care or call emergency services.”

5. Beta/waitlist section
Title:
“Help shape Norovia.”

Text:
“We’re inviting early users to test the first version and share what feels useful, unclear, or missing.”

Input:
Email address field

Button:
“Request beta access”

Small text:
“No spam. Just beta updates and early access.”

6. Footer disclaimer
“Norovia is not medical care and does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional medical advice. If you are experiencing severe or concerning symptoms, contact a clinician or emergency services.”

Design requirements:
- Mobile-first responsive layout
- Calm ivory/off-white background
- Soft cards with rounded corners
- Muted accent color, not bright red except subtle flare section
- Clean spacing
- Modern typography
- CTA visible near top and near bottom
- Looks premium but simple
- No stock photos for now
- No complex animations
- Keep code clean and easy to edit

Engineering requirements:
- Implement as a simple landing page in the existing Replit project
- Do not break existing app routing
- If this is a React web project, create/update the landing page component only
- If email capture backend is not set up, make the form visually functional and show a simple “Thank you — you’re on the list” message on submit
- Do not add external dependencies unless absolutely necessary
- Keep all copy easy to edit in one place if possible

Deliverable:
Build the landing page and then show me:
1. What file(s) you changed
2. A short summary of what you implemented
3. Any placeholder logic used for the email form
